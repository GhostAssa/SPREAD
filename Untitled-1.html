Union Leaders Demand Transparency in Fee Allocation

By Adebayo T.

Published Oct 24, 2024

The student union has issued an ultimatum to the university administration, demanding a full public audit of the recently introduced "Campus Development Fee" within the next 48 hours.

Tensions reached a boiling point on Tuesday afternoon when over 500 students gathered outside the administrative block. The demonstration, organized by the Student Representative Council (SRC), was sparked by leaked documents suggesting that a significant portion of the new fees was being diverted from the promised library renovations to executive travel expenses.

"We are not ATMs"

"We were told this fee was essential for upgrading our failing IT infrastructure and expanding study spaces," said SRC President, Amara Nwosu, speaking to the crowd through a megaphone. "Instead, we are seeing zero improvements while management authorizes luxury retreats. We are students, not ATMs."

"The lack of transparency is no longer an oversight; it's a deliberate strategy to avoid accountability." - Amara Nwosu

University spokesperson, Dr. Elias Mensah, released a brief statement late Tuesday denying the allegations of misappropriation. He stated that the leaked documents were "preliminary drafts" and did not reflect the final budget allocations. He assured students that a detailed breakdown would be provided at the end of the fiscal quarter.

However, the union leadership remains unconvinced, pointing out that previous promises of financial transparency have repeatedly fallen through.

  • 3
