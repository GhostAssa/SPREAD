# Project Brief: SPREAD — Campus News, Checked First

## 1. Project Vision
**Spread** is a high-energy digital broadcast platform designed for university campuses, specifically the University of Ibadan. It serves as a "Human-Verified" news ecosystem, bridging the gap between viral campus rumors and verified truth. The identity is rooted in a "Nigeria-core" digital collage aesthetic, emphasizing authenticity, transparency, and high-impact communication.

## 2. Core Value Proposition
- **Human-Verified:** A strict editorial protocol where every piece of news is checked by a human before it is broadcast.
- **Anonymous Reporting:** A secure "Tip Drop" system that allows students to report incidents without requiring accounts or names.
- **High-Energy Transparency:** Making the verification process visible through motion, tickers, and "TV-set" motifs.

## 3. Visual Identity (Lagos Lo-Fi)
- **Primary Typeface:** Bricolage Grotesque (Bold, high-impact headlines; functional body text).
- **Secondary Motif Fonts:** Caveat Brush, Permanent Marker (Used for "Human-Verified" annotations and digital graffiti).
- **Color Palette:**
  - **Surface:** Sand (#E4E0D4) and Cream (#F4F1E7) for a tactile, lo-fi feel.
  - **Accents:** High-contrast pops of Clay (Orange), Pink, Teal, and Ink-Band (Dark Navy).
- **Visual Language:** Custom SVG motifs (swooshes, wave bands, signal tracks), wireframe globes, and "TV-set" container frames for content.

## 4. Key Features & Experience
- **Broadcast Hero:** A high-impact entry point with real-time "Checked Story" counters and parallax motif clusters.
- **Live News Feed:** A staggered grid of "On Air" signal news cards, each framed in custom motifs.
- **News Detail (Deep Dive):** Long-form reading experience with massive typography and motif-framed sidebars.
- **The Blueprint (Process):** An interactive scroll-choreographed timeline showing the verification journey from "Tip" to "Broadcast."
- **The Tip Drop:** A secure submission form for anonymous "Intel" with category classification (Facilities, Safety, Academics, etc.).
- **Live Marquee:** A high-contrast scrolling ticker for real-time campus alerts and confirmed facts.

## 5. Technical Specification
- **Framework:** Next.js for performance and SEO.
- **Motion:** Scroll-triggered reveals, parallax effects, and marquee animations using CSS and vanilla JavaScript Intersection Observers.
- **Typography:** Google Fonts integration (Bricolage Grotesque, Caveat Brush, Permanent Marker).
- **Responsiveness:** Fluid scaling for mobile-first campus usage and wide-screen desktop broadcasting.

## 6. Success Metrics
- **Verification Velocity:** Time elapsed from tip submission to "Human-Verified" broadcast.
- **Accuracy Rate:** Percentage of verified vs. debunked stories.
- **Engagement:** Frequency of anonymous tips and community reliance on the platform as a source of truth.
